# Street Portraits

A collection of candid and posed portraits taken in urban environments, exploring the relationship between individuals and their surroundings.

## Philosophy

Street photography is about finding the extraordinary in the ordinary. These portraits capture fleeting moments of human connection and solitude.

## Approach

Each portrait tells a story - sometimes of a brief encounter, sometimes of a longer conversation that led to collaboration between photographer and subject.
