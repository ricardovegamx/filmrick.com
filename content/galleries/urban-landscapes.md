# Urban Landscapes

This collection explores the intersection of human-made structures and natural light in urban environments. Each photograph captures a moment where architecture becomes art.

## The Vision

Through careful composition and attention to geometric forms, these images reveal the hidden beauty in everyday urban scenes.

<Quote author="Desmond Morris">
The city is not a concrete jungle, it is a human zoo.
</Quote>

## Technical Approach

- **Camera**: Leica M10 Monochrom
- **Lenses**: 35mm f/2 Summicron, 50mm f/1.4 Summilux
- **Film**: Digital monochrome sensor
- **Processing**: Minimal adjustment, emphasis on natural contrast
