# About FILMRICK

I'm a photographer based in Tokyo, Japan, with a passion for capturing the quiet moments in urban environments. My work focuses on the intersection of architecture, light, and human presence in the modern city.

## Background

With over a decade of experience in photography, I've developed a minimalist approach that emphasizes composition, contrast, and storytelling through images.

## Philosophy

Photography is about more than just documenting reality - it's about revealing the poetry hidden in everyday moments.

## Get in Touch

For inquiries about prints, collaborations, or just to say hello, you can reach me through my [Substack newsletter](https://filmrick.substack.com) where I share stories about photography, travel, and the creative process.
